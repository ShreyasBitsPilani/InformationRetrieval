Types of NFL Bets You Can Place

When diving into the world of NFL betting, understanding the various types of NFL bets is essential for developing effective strategies. Each wager type offers unique ways to engage with the game, providing you with a range of betting options to choose from. Whether you’re new to betting or looking to refine your approach, knowing these wager types can enhance your overall betting experience.

Moneylines

Moneyline bets are among the simplest wager types available. When you place a moneyline bet, you simply choose which team will win the game. The odds reflect the probability of each team winning, with favorites requiring a larger stake for a smaller return and underdogs providing the chance for a higher payout on smaller bets.

Spreads

Spread betting ranks as one of the most popular betting options in NFL markets. In this format, a point spread is established to level the playing field between two teams. You can bet on whether a favored team will win by more than the designated spread, or if the underdog will stay within the spread. This method introduces an extra layer of excitement to the wager.

Totals (Over/Under)

Betting on totals, often referred to as Over/Under bets, involves predicting the combined score of both teams. You can wager whether the total points scored will go above or below the bookmaker’s set line. This type of bet allows you to focus on the scoring potential of both teams, regardless of the match outcome.

Props & Parlays

Proposition bets, or props, offer unique wagering options by focusing on specific game or player outcomes. These can range from individual player performance stats to specific events occurring during the game. Parlays, on the other hand, combine multiple wagers into one bet, allowing for higher payouts if all selections are correct. Both types of bets add versatility to your overall betting strategy.
